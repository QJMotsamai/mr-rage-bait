Privacy Policy

Effective 19 August 2026 · Last updated 19 August 2026

The chatbot is sarcastic. This policy is not. It explains what Mr Rage Bait collects when you use the service, including when you create an account or pay.

1. Who we are

Mr Rage Bait is operated by QJ Motsamai in South Africa (“we”, “us”). This policy applies to the website, chat service, accounts, and billing features at this domain.

Contact for privacy requests: QJ Motsamai on LinkedIn.

2. The short version

  • You can try the chat as a guest. We then only store a random cookie and a daily message count.
  • If you sign in, we store your email, password hash or Google id, country guess, and plan.
  • We do not keep a permanent archive of your chat transcripts.
  • Messages you send are processed by Google Gemini so the character can reply, then discarded from our database.
  • If you pay, Stripe or Paystack see your identity and charge you in your local currency. We never receive your full card number.
  • The owner dashboard shows who signed in, who opened checkout, and who paid. That is how we tell a willing buyer from a tourist.

3. Information we collect

Account data. Email address, display name, hashed password, Google account id if you use Google sign-in, plan (free or pro), and timestamps for signup and last visit.

Approximate location. Country, timezone, and browser language so we can show and charge the right local currency. We do not collect GPS.

Usage data. How many messages you sent today, total messages, and billing events such as “opened upgrade”, “started checkout”, “paid”, or “cancelled”.

Theme preference. If you are on Pro, we store which visual theme you chose (Acid Noir, Red Alert, or Midnight Blue). Free accounts always use Acid Noir.

Chat content. Your latest messages are sent to our server only long enough to call Google Gemini and return a reply. We do not store the conversation in our user database. A short live history stays in your browser until you refresh or hit Rage Refund.

Attachments. Files you attach are read in memory to send to Gemini and are not written to disk by this app.

Technical data. IP address (used by the host and payment provider), browser type, and essential cookies.

Payment data. Handled by Stripe and/or Paystack. They receive your email, country, the amount, and the currency. They may collect billing name, card or EFT details, and fraud signals. We store only their customer or subscription references, the currency charged, and whether you are Pro.

4. Why we use it

  • To run the chatbot you asked for.
  • To create and secure your account.
  • To enforce free daily limits and unlock Pro.
  • To identify the person who is willing to buy and charge them in their local currency.
  • To prevent abuse, spam, and fraud.
  • To meet tax, accounting, and legal duties.
  • To answer support and privacy requests.

Under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), we process this information because it is necessary to perform the contract you enter by creating an account or paying, because we have a legitimate interest in running and securing the service, and where required because the law says we must keep transaction records. Where consent is needed, you give it by signing in or starting checkout.

5. Who else sees it

  • Google Gemini / Google LLC — processes chat text and attachments to generate replies. If you use Google sign-in, Google also authenticates you.
  • Render or any host we deploy on — stores the running app and the account database.
  • Stripe and/or Paystack — process payments, detect fraud, and settle funds. They are responsible for card data.
  • You, the account holder — can see your own email and plan in the app.
  • QJ Motsamai — can see account and billing events in the private admin board, and in the Stripe or Paystack dashboard.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your chats to train a public advertising profile. We do not run third-party advertising cookies.

6. Cookies

We only use essential cookies:

  • a guest id so free limits work before you sign in;
  • a signed session cookie after you sign in;
  • a short-lived Google sign-in state cookie;
  • an admin cookie if the owner signs into the private board.

These cookies are required for the service to function. There is no advertising cookie wall.

7. How long we keep it

  • Guest ids and daily counts: about 14 days of usage counters, cookie up to 180 days.
  • Accounts: until you ask us to delete them, or they stay inactive and we later purge unused free accounts.
  • Billing events and paid invoices: kept as long as tax and accounting law in South Africa requires, usually at least five years for transaction records.
  • Chat content: not stored in our database after the reply is sent.

8. Your rights

If you are in South Africa, you have rights under POPIA, including to access your information, correct it, object to certain processing, and request deletion where we do not have to keep it. People in the UK/EU have similar rights under UK GDPR / GDPR.

To use them, contact QJ Motsamai via LinkedIn and say you are writing about Mr Rage Bait privacy. We may need to verify that the account is yours. You can also delete your browser cookies, sign out, and stop using the service. Paid subscriptions must be cancelled with the payment provider if you want charges to stop.

9. Children

The service is a parody product with an adult tone. You must be at least 16 to create an account. You must be at least 18 to buy Pro. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

10. Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, not as plain text. Session tokens are stored hashed. Gemini API keys stay on the server. Payment cards never touch our server. No method of transmission is perfect; do not send secrets you cannot afford to type into a joke chatbot.

11. International transfers

Your information may be processed in South Africa and in other countries where Google, Stripe, Paystack, or our host operate, including the United States and the European Union. We only use established providers and only for the purposes above.

12. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use after that date means you accept the updated policy.
