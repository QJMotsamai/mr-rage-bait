WHAT THIS DOES
--------------
Right now, pasting your link into WhatsApp shows a grey box with
no picture. After this it shows your artwork, your headline and
your description - which is the difference between people
clicking and people scrolling past.

Works on WhatsApp, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Telegram and iMessage.

It also gives you a proper icon in the browser tab, and a real
icon if someone adds the site to their phone's home screen.

And your /admin page is now marked "do not index", so it will
not turn up in Google results.


UPLOAD (one commit)
-------------------
1. github.com/QJMotsamai/mr-rage-bait  ->  click  public
2. Add file -> Upload files
3. Drag ALL EIGHT files in
4. Commit message:  Add link preview image and app icons
5. Commit changes

Because you go into the public folder first, everything lands in
the right place automatically.

Nothing to change on Render or Paystack.
No new environment variables. No new packages.
