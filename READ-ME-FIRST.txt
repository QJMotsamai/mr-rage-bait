THE FIX
-------
Before: opening the menu made the top bar grow taller and pushed
the chat down. On a shorter phone the bottom of the menu -
including Privacy and Terms under Sign out - got squeezed out of
sight, and it did not look like a menu at all.

Now: the menu floats ON TOP of the chat, the chat behind it is
dimmed, and the menu scrolls on its own if the phone is small.
Privacy and Terms are always reachable.

Tap the dimmed area, the X, or press Escape to close it.

Tested on a small 360x640 phone: the whole menu fits.

Not touched: Paystack, database, package.json, render.yaml, keys.
No new environment variables. No new packages.
The computer layout is unchanged.
